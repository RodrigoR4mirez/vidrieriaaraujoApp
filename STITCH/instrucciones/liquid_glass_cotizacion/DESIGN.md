## Brand & Style

Liquid Glass Cotización combines hyper-realistic **Glassmorphism** with high-precision enterprise utility. Built for professional architectural and industrial quoting (glazing, aluminum fabrication, materials estimation), the visual identity balances technical confidence with an ethereal, optical-depth feel.

### Personality & Values
- **Optical Precision:** Reflected through crisp translucent glass panels, internal light refractions, and sub-pixel high-contrast white rim lighting.
- **Modern Industrial Cleanliness:** Moving beyond flat grey software into a vibrant, airy, clean-room atmosphere dominated by structural blues and frosty layers.
- **Tangible Tactility:** Buttons and interactive pills behave like molded acrylic slabs with specular highlights and pressurized feedback states.

### Emotional Response
Users should feel clarity, administrative speed, and absolute trust in the calculation integrity without the visual fatigue typical of legacy enterprise ERP sheets.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around liquid refraction: clear water whites, cool atmospheric slate greys, and high-energy electric blues.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary (`#1d6ae5`):** The functional heartbeat. Used for hero numerical summaries, critical call-to-actions, active segment states, and category indicator nodes.
- **Secondary (`#3b82f6`):** Highlights and gradient top-stops that give liquid buttons their domed luminance.
- **Tertiary / Destructive (`#ef4444`):** Soft rose-tinted frosted surfaces with vibrant crimson glyphs for non-disruptive item deletion.
- **Neutrals (`#0f172a`, `#334155`, `#64748b`, `#94a3b8`):** Deep charcoal slate headings down to subtle muted metadata captions.
- **Canvas Base:** Non-flat dynamic backdrop shifting diagonally from `#f1f5fa` through `#e2e8f0` down to `#dbe4ef`.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric proportions and humanist curves to contrast against rigid tabular data.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Invoice / Serial Identifiers (`display-hero`):** Extra-bold weight with tight tracking creates an anchor point for formal document verification.
- **Metric Totals (`display-hero-mobile` to `headline-lg`):** Numeric figures are rendered in ultra-bold weight to be legible at an immediate glance.
- **Headers & Labels (`label-caps` / `label-micro`):** Upper-case, track-spaced labels (10px–11px) serve as category dividers and input micro-headers, creating structural organization without heavy visual borders.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas is constructed as a centralized floating application stage on top of an atmospheric radial canvas.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Canvas Shell:** Centered layout locked at a maximum width of `80rem` (1280px / `max-w-7xl`) with generous padding (`2rem` / `p-8` on desktop, `1rem` on mobile).
- **Asymmetric Grid System:** Uses a 12-column grid structure where:
  - **Left Form Rail:** Spans 4 columns (`col-span-4`) containing operational input builders, steppers, and pill triggers.
  - **Right Summary Stage:** Spans 8 columns (`col-span-8`) housing line item tables, summary totals, and final validation workflows.
- **Inner Panel Rhythm:** Sub-cards and form blocks are uniformly separated by `1.75rem` (`gap-7` / `space-y-7`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation is produced through optical simulation: multi-layer frosted glass, lens refraction, and directional specular rim lights rather than pure shadow drops.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Base Master Stage (`.glass-panel`):**
   - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.62)`
   - Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(180%)`
   - Perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
   - Light Injection: Top-edge specular inner glow `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with dual soft ambient occlusion `0 20px 45px -15px rgba(0, 35, 90, 0.08)`.

2. **Sub-Containers & Group Cards (`.glass-card`):**
   - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.48)`
   - Filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`
   - Perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 25px -8px rgba(30, 60, 110, 0.06), inset 0 1.5px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`.

3. **Liquid Floating Actions (`.liquid-btn-blue`):**
   - Gradient Fill: Vertical 3-stop `linear-gradient(180deg, #3b82f6 0%, #2563eb 55%, #1d4ed8 100%)`
   - Shadow: Atmospheric colored glow `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.45)` paired with double interior bevels `inset 0 2px 3px rgba(255,255,255,0.4)` and `inset 0 -2px 4px rgba(0,25,120,0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, pebble-smooth corner radius philosophy (`roundedness: 2` base) calibrated across scale hierarchies:

- **Master Glass App Panel:** `1.5rem` to `2rem` (`rounded-3xl` / 24px–32px).
- **Sub-cards & Headers:** `1rem` to `1.25rem` (`rounded-2xl` / 16px–20px).
- **Controls, Input Fields & Steppers:** `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl` / 12px) ensuring tactile grip.
- **Segment Toggles & Pill Badges:** Full-pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for status indicators and micro-actions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Liquid Primary (`.liquid-btn-blue`):** Pill or rounded-xl buttons with multi-tier blue gradients, white top bevel highlights, deep blue inner shadows, and dynamic translateY transitions (`-1.5px` hover, `+0.5px` active).
- **Liquid Clear (`.liquid-btn-clear`):** Ultra-light frosted glass pill button with white inner rim and high-contrast slate text; used for item-level editing and reset actions.
- **Danger Pill (`.btn-glass-danger`):** Translucent crimson background (`rgba(254, 242, 242, 0.85)`) with a subtle red border and hover scale effect.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Glass Input Capsule (`.glass-input`):** Semi-opaque background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70)`) with micro-labels pinned at the top-left, border transition to vibrant blue on focus, and an atmospheric focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).
- **Quantity Steppers:** Paired square-rounded (`w-11 h-11`) glass buttons enclosing a static high-contrast counter module.

### Pill Thickness Selectors & Segment Bars
- **Multi-Segment Switcher:** Pill-shaped recessed track (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.65)`) holding floating active state capsules.
- **Dimension Selector Buttons:** 4-column compact grid of pills; active items receive primary liquid gradients while inactive items sit in semi-translucent frosted glass.

### Tables & Cotización Lists
- Grouped inside nested glass cards with colored category tags (blue accent dot with uppercase group name).
- Minimalist hairline dividers (`border-slate-100/80`) replacing heavy table borders.
- Hover-activated row highlight (`hover:bg-white/30`) for comfortable scanning.
